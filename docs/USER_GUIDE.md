# User Guide (plain English)

**What this website does:** you give it a spreadsheet of your sales, orders or reviews. It tells you **what is going wrong, why it is probably happening, and what you can do about it**.

## 1. Try it first (no account)
Open the home page and press **Explore demo**. A yellow **DEMO MODE** bar shows the 500-order limit. You can look at charts, check up to 10 orders and look at 3 sellers.

## 2. Create your account
Press **Create free account**. Use your name, an email and a password with at least 10 characters, letters and a number. The coloured bars under the password show if it is strong enough.

## 3. The menu (left side)
| Menu item | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your top 3 things to work on, shortcuts and recent activity |
| **My Data** | Upload a file, check it, match columns, analyse, create a PDF report |
| **Insights & Advice** | Every finding as **Problem → Why it is happening → What you can do** |
| **Ask a Question** | Type a question; answers come from the real numbers |
| **Order Risk Check** | Is an order likely to arrive late? What to do |
| **What-If Planner** | “What if deliveries were 2 days faster?” |
| **Reports & History** | Download earlier PDF reports; reopen saved results |
| **Settings** | Change your name or password |
| *More tools (advanced)* | The original research pages, for people who want the details |

## 4. From file to advice in 4 steps (My Data)
1. **Upload & check** – CSV, Excel or JSONL. We tell you rows, columns and data quality in simple words.
2. **Match your columns** – we guess which column is date, product, price… Press **Looks right — continue**, or correct a column first.
3. **Analyse** – one button runs everything your file supports. Analyses that are not possible are listed with the reason.
4. **Advice & report** – press **See advice & recommendations**, or **Create PDF report** (the report has a section with the same Problem / Why / What-to-do advice).

## 5. How to read an advice card
- **Red – The problem:** what is wrong, with real numbers.
- **Orange – Why it is happening:** what the data shows is behind it.
- **Green – What you can do:** practical steps.
- **Fix first / Worth doing / Good news** tells you the priority. “Good news” means something is fine and you do not need to spend effort there.
- **Show the numbers behind this** lists the figures used. The label at the bottom (Strong evidence / Some evidence / Early signal) tells you how much data supports the card.

## 6. Good to know
- Advice shows **patterns**, not guaranteed causes. Try a fix on a small scale first and compare results.
- Small files give weaker evidence; the card says so.
- Your files are private. Only you (and the administrator) can open them.

## Demo mode: check your own data (first 500 rows only)
On the **Explore demo** page, use *Check your own data* to upload a `.csv` or `.xlsx` file (up to 5 MB). Demo mode reads **only the first 500 rows** and shows a clear notice such as *"Data provided is limited to 500 rows only. Your file has 1,234 rows; only the first 500 rows were used."* Nothing is saved. A guest may check up to 5 files per session. Registered users analyse the full file.

## Leaving the application safely
* Pressing the browser **Back** button while signed in **signs you out**; you must log in again. Pages are never cached.
* If work is running (an upload, analysis, report, prediction...), the app asks **"Do you really want to exit?"** with **Yes** and **No**. *Yes* signs you out and opens the home page; *No* keeps you on the page. The same question appears when you click a link or the Log-out button while work is running.
