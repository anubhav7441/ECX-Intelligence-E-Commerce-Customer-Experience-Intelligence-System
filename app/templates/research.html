Limitations. Observational, historical (2016-2018) data from one marketplace; correlation is not causation. Only customers who left a review are analysed (selection bias). Many late-order reviews pre-date the recorded delivery. Review text exists for only about 41% of reviews and is Portuguese; complaint labels come from rules checked on a 150-review sample labelled by one annotator, and sentiment labels are derived from star ratings. Multi-item orders have one review and one seller is attributed per order. Repeat purchasing is rare (about 3% of customers), limiting Q10. Model scores are modest and not calibrated probabilities; the system is for risk monitoring, not for decisions about individual customers.
