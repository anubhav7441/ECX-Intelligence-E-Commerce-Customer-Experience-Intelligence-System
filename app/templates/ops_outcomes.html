Did the action work?

How we measure. We compare the rate in the same number of days before and after the action date, and subtract what happened to all other orders over the same period (difference-in-differences). It says "improved" or "worsened" only when the whole 95% interval is on one side of zero. This is not a randomised experiment, so treat it as strong evidence, not proof. Fixes also take time to reach customers.
